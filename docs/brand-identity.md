## 1. Who we are

**Sutura Works Limited** is a Nigerian consultancy that builds the operational structure a
growing organisation needs and rarely has time to write down: audits, policies and procedures,
governance and compliance structure, then training and run-in until the work is embedded.

- **Registered:** RC 8908551, incorporated October 2025
- **Based in:** Abuja and Kano, Nigeria
- **Sectors served to date:** healthcare, education, manufacturing
- **Website:** suturaworks.com

### The name

*Sutura* is the Hausa word for covering and protection. That is the whole idea: a system exists
to cover the people working inside it.

### Positioning

| | |
|---|---|
| **Tagline** | Empowering organisations. |
| **Signature line** | Written for the floor. Not the shelf. |
| **Category descriptor** | Governance and operational infrastructure |
| **Method** | The Operational Infrastructure Methodology: Diagnose, Document, Embed. Three stages, in order, every time. |

### Vision

An organisation that is not held together by one person's memory, where the system carries the
weight and the people inside it are covered. We want the structure we build to outlast us: if a
client can run it, review it and revise it without calling us back, the engagement worked.

### Values

1. **Sutura.** Covering and protection. A system exists to cover the people working inside it.
2. **Lane discipline.** We say plainly where our work ends and where a lawyer or an accountant begins.
3. **Evidence over assumption.** We never sell a template pack as a diagnosis.

### Operating principles (what we will not do)

1. **No inspection theatre.** We do not write documents to pass an inspection and then leave.
2. **No template packs.** Every document starts inside the client's operation.
3. **No advice outside our lane.** Where the work touches law or finance, we say so and hand it on.

## 2. The mark

The mark folds an **S** into a **W** and shelters a single **sage chevron** inside it. That is
the firm in one shape: structure that covers the people working inside it.

It is built from three separate shapes: the upper S form, the lower W form and the chevron.
On the website the three rise into place one after another (see section 6).

### Versions

| Version | Use | File |
|---|---|---|
| White mark, sage chevron | On deep forest and other dark grounds | `assets/mark-white.png` |
| Deep green mark, sage chevron | On pale sage, mist or paper | `assets/mark-dark.png` |
| Full logo on dark (mark and wordmark) | Social banners, documents, presentations | `assets/logo-full-dark.png` |
| Full logo on light (mark and wordmark) | Letterheads, print, light documents | `assets/logo-full-light.png` |
| Favicon | Browser tabs and app icons | `assets/favicon.png`, `assets/favicon-32.png` |

### Wordmark

**SUTURA** in bold capitals followed by **WORKS LIMITED** in regular capitals, letter-spaced,
set below the mark or beside it. Always use the supplied logo files; do not retype the wordmark.

### Rules

- **Clear space:** at least the height of the chevron on every side.
- **Minimum size:** 24px on screen, 10mm in print.
- **Proportion:** portrait, about 0.8 wide to 1 tall. Never stretch it to square.
- **Never** recolour the chevron.
- **Never** place the mark straight onto a photograph. Put a solid panel behind it first.
- **Mark with wordmark** wherever there is room. The mark alone is for icons, avatars and favicons.

| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Panel | `#13251F` | Raised panels and alternate dark bands |

### Colour rules

- The brand is **dark first**: deep forest is the default ground, with pale sage cards and one
  sage band for emphasis.
- Body text on dark is muted; headings on dark are mist.
- Sage on deep forest is for labels, numbers and accents only, never body text.
- On pale sage or sage, all text is ink.

## 5. Voice

**Plain, direct and human.** We write for the person holding the clipboard, not for the shelf.

### We write

- Short sentences that each say one thing.
- What the client ends up with, not our credentials.
- The reasoning, so the reader can follow how we got there.
- Lists that vary in length. Two items when there are two.

### We avoid

- Em dashes and en dashes.
- The Oxford comma.
- Taglines and sales language that could belong to any firm.
- Naming clients or employers anywhere we describe our work in public.

### Spelling

British English throughout: organisation, programme, licence (noun), favour.

## 6. Graphic language

- **Square corners** everywhere: buttons, cards, panels and form fields.
- **Hairlines:** 1px rules separate sections and list items rather than boxes or shadows.
- **Outlined diamonds:** squares turned 45 degrees with a thin outline and no fill. Used behind
  the mark in the hero and as the frames for the three method steps.
- **Diamond bullets:** small outlined diamonds mark list items.
- **Ghost mark:** a very large copy of the mark at about 6% opacity, cropped by the page edge,
  in the footer.
- **Motion:** on the website hero, the diamonds fade in, then the W, the S and the chevron rise
  into place in turn (about two seconds, once). Motion is switched off for visitors who ask for
  reduced motion.
