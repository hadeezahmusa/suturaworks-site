How do people really start and run a business in Nigeria?

We are researching the experiences of people starting and operating businesses in Nigeria, and of the professionals who help them formalise, manage or resolve business problems.

The aim is to understand where information is hard to find or hard to trust, what goes wrong and what support would make the journey easier. This is research, not a test. Positive, negative and mixed experiences are all welcome, and there are no right or wrong answers.
