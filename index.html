How we work

Three stages. In order. Every time.

The Operational Infrastructure Methodology. Nothing gets drafted until we have seen how the operation really runs, and nothing is finished until people are using it.

  1. 01

    Diagnose

    See the real workflow, not the described one. Interviews at every level, observation on the floor and a discovery report you can act on even if we stop there.

  2. 02

    Document

    Write from what we found, not from a template library. Plain language, numbered and version controlled so anyone can tell which copy is current.

  3. 03

    Embed

    Train the people who will use it, run it in and correct what did not survive the working day. A procedure nobody follows is a procedure nobody paid for.

Who we are

For organisations that have outgrown one person’s memory.

Sutura Works is a Nigerian consultancy. Sutura is Hausa for covering and protection, and that is the whole idea: a system exists to cover the people working inside it.

Most growing organisations run on goodwill and a few people who know where everything is. That works until someone is on leave, an inspector arrives or a key member of staff moves on. We turn that knowledge into structure the organisation owns.

We want the structure we build to outlast us. If a client can run it, review it and revise it without calling us back, the engagement worked.
