Covering; protection. A system exists to cover the people working inside it.

1.0

Purpose

A Nigerian consultancy for organisations that have outgrown one person’s memory.

Most growing organisations run on goodwill and a few people who know where everything is. That works until someone is on leave, an inspector arrives or a key member of staff moves on. We turn that knowledge into structure the organisation owns.

We want the structure we build to outlast us. If a client can run it, review it and revise it without calling us back, the engagement worked.

2.0

Principles

  1. 2.1

    Covering and protection

    A system exists to cover the people working inside it. That is the whole idea behind the name.

  2. 2.2

    Lane discipline

    We say plainly where our work ends and where a lawyer or an accountant begins.

  3. 2.3

    Evidence over assumption

    We never sell a template pack as a diagnosis. Every document starts from what we found inside your operation.

4.0

Procedure

Every engagement follows the same path. We do not skip a step to save time, because the step we skip is the one that fails later.

  1. Step 01

    Diagnose

    We sit inside the operation first. Interviews at every level, observation of the real workflow and a gap analysis you could act on alone.

  2. Step 02

    Document

    Policies, SOPs, forms and process maps drafted from what we found. Plain language, numbered and version controlled, written for the person holding the clipboard.

  3. Step 03

    Embed

    Training for the people who will use the documents, a handover pack and a review after go live to fix whatever did not survive the working day.
